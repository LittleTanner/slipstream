The rules

Three things run down. Only your legs come back for free.

LegsWind is what costs you. Sit on a wheel and they come back.
FoodDrains every stage and never returns on its own. Eat before you need to.
FluidYou sweat harder climbing, and harder still in the peloton where there is no air.
GutYou only take on so much at once. Grab two things together and you waste the second.
WindA crosswind moves the shelter sideways. Get on the right side of the wheel or ride in the gutter.
The jumpAlternate the L and R pedals to lift the pace, faster for more. Two strokes on the same side is a missed pedal and it costs you. Hold both to soft pedal and recover.
The pelotonThey only chase what scares them. Go up the road before you are worth chasing.
Penalties

Commissaires judge you and your rivals alike. Lower divisions forgive a second offence; Division 1 forgives nothing.

Fines come out of your prize money. Time penalties are added to your finishing time. Losing a sprint means you take no points and no money at that line.
