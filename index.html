Three things run down. Only your legs come back for free.

LegsWind is what costs you. Sit on a wheel and they come back.
FuelDrains every stage and never returns on its own. Eat before you need to.
FluidYou sweat harder climbing, and harder still in the bunch where there is no air.
GutYou only take on so much at once. Grab two things together and you waste the second.
WindA crosswind moves the shelter sideways. Get on the right side of the wheel or ride in the gutter.
The jumpAlternate the L and R pedals to go above tempo, faster for more. Two strokes on the same side is a missed pedal and it costs you. Hold both to coast and recover.
The bunchThey only chase what scares them. Go up the road before you are worth chasing.
